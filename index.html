Odenak Affordable Housing Program

Affordable
Design

Odenak is creating a community designed to be more accessible, diverse and welcoming with 41% of units offered below market rent. The affordable design strategy supports people who have historically faced barriers to adequate housing, including Indigenous Peoples, veterans, newcomers, refugees, adults with cognitive disabilities, and women and children.

Affordable Living
at Odenak

Ideally positioned along the Confederation LRT Line and nestled at the river’s edge, Odenak offers seamless access to everything the capital has to offer. Two distinct residences — Odenak East and West — deliver a thoughtfully curated collection of studios to three-bedroom suites, featuring refined interiors, modern finishes, and amenities designed around the way you live. More than just a place to call home, Odenak is a vibrant mixed-use community built for connection — to the city, to your neighbours, and to a sustainable future.

Amenities

At Odenak, every space has been designed with your lifestyle in mind. From fully equipped gyms and coworking lounges to terraced community gardens, outdoor BBQ terraces, and a one-of-a-kind community bike shop — life here goes well beyond your suite. Thoughtfully crafted gathering spaces, a dedicated kids’ club, and programming designed to bring residents together makes Odenak a place you’ll truly want to come home to.

Suite Features

Every Odenak suite is finished with quality materials and elevated details you’ll notice from the moment you walk in. Choose from two curated palettes each bringing its own warmth and character to your home. Wood-look flooring flows throughout, complemented by quartz countertops, porcelain tile, and a full suite of stainless-steel appliances. In-suite laundry make everyday living effortless.

Program Details & Eligibility

As part of the screening process, you will be required to demonstrate your household income does not exceed the maximum income limits, in addition to meeting other screening requirements.

Submission of an application does not guarantee eligibility, approval, selection, tenancy, or a specific unit type. All applications are subject to verification of information, program eligibility requirements, unit availability, and landlord screening criteria.

Due to anticipated demand, application review times may vary. Eligible applicants will be contacted if selected to proceed to the next stage of the screening process.

Priority Applicant Groups

Priority consideration may be given to households that fall within National Housing Strategy (NHS) Priority Populations in accordance with applicable funding requirements. Membership within a priority population does not guarantee selection or tenancy.

  1. Women and children
  2. Indigenous groups
  3. Veterans
  4. Newcomers/recent immigrants
  5. Adults with cognitive disabilities

Income-Based Eligibility

Applicants must declare all income, benefits, and assets as part of the application.

To qualify, a household’s income must fall within the income parameters established for the Affordable Housing Program and applicable Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC) requirements.

To determine eligibility, add together the Line 23600 income from the most recent Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Notice of Assessment for all household members aged 16 and older.

Applicants must provide satisfactory documentation to verify household income, household composition, residency status, and program eligibility. Odenak reserves the right to request additional documentation at any stage of the application process.

Any false, incomplete, or misleading information may result in the rejection of an application or termination of eligibility under the Affordable Housing Program. Odenak reserves the right to reject, cancel, or withdraw an application or offer if information is found to be false, misleading, incomplete, or fraudulent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Apply

  • Household income must be below the applicable income limit.
  • Priority consideration may be given to certain applicant groups.
  • Utilities and parking are not included.

What’s Included In Your Monthly Rent?

  • In-suite laundry
  • Access to building amenities
  • Professional on-site property management

What’s Not Included In Your Monthly Rent?

  • Hydro Ottawa
  • Lebreton Community Utility (heating and cooling) charges
  • Internet and telecommunication services
  • Storage lockers
  • Optional resident services

Is Parking Available?

Parking will not be available. The community is located steps from Pimisi Station and offers convenient access to public transit, cycling infrastructure, and active transportation routes.

Do I Qualify If My Income Is Below The Limit?

Meeting the income requirements does not guarantee approval or unit availability.

How Is Household Income Calculated?

Income is based on Line 23600 of the CRA Notice of Assessment for all household members aged 16 and older.

How Much Can My Rent Be Increased Annually?

Rent increases, if applicable, will be implemented in accordance with the Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, applicable Affordable Housing Program requirements, and any obligations contained within governing funding agreements.

Occupancy Standards

Household occupancy must comply with applicable program requirements, municipal standards, and Odenak occupancy policies in effect at the time of application and occupancy.

Waitlist And Selection Process

Where the number of eligible applicants exceeds available units, Odenak may maintain a waitlist or use a selection process consistent with program requirements and applicable funding agreements.

Eligibility for the Affordable Housing Program does not guarantee the availability of a particular unit type, floor plan, building location, view, orientation, or move-in date.

Odenak reserves the right to refuse any application that does not meet program requirements, eligibility criteria, occupancy standards, or applicable funding restrictions.

Principal Residence Requirement

Approved applicants must occupy the rental unit as their principal residence and may be required to certify continued occupancy from time to time.
